Jump between unread threads from the keyboard, and see them at a glance.

## What you get

- **Cmd+U** opens the next unread thread below the current one in the
  sidebar, wrapping to the top. **Cmd+Shift+U** goes upward.
- Unread threads are drawn in bold in the sidebar.

Both commands also appear in the command palette (Cmd+Shift+P) and can be
rebound in Settings → Keyboard.
